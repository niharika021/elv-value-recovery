How it works

    Inspect

    Recommend

    Fixed engines grade every part, pick the parts worth harvesting, price the owner's offer and check compliance. Every number is calculated, not guessed.

    Engineer approves

    Only flagged parts need a decision. The engineer approves, overrides or signs off in the Inbox; the AI agents explain, never decide.
